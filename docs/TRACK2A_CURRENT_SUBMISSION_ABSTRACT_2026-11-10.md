# PolicyProof AI — current Track 2a research abstract (draft, 10 November 2026)

PolicyProof AI is a reproducible multilingual safety benchmark and research toolkit for evidence-grounded policy question answering, developed for Lexsi Track 2a. We evaluate structured response validity, citation identifiers, abstention on unsupported questions, and resistance to adversarial instructions embedded in fictional policy documents across English, Norwegian and Arabic.

We used CuratorKIT 0.1.22 to validate and curate synthetic examples and AuditKIT 1.1.3 for repeatable strict scoring. Our publicly released dataset includes the original 48-case development material and a separately curated 72-case adversarial challenge. A later private original-model run saved 72 challenge responses with source-linked local evaluation evidence; strict reference match was 11/72, JSON validity 58/72 and canonical unsupported-question abstention 6/12. This is a synthetic strict-reference result, not independent semantic grading or robust evidence of attack success.

We also conducted an owner-reported 40-step native AlignTune LoRA fine-tuning trial on a distinct 78-example training set. The resulting private adapter has verified tensor structure and file hashes, but neither GPU execution nor the exact base-model revision loaded during training is independently attested. A fresh matched, development-only comparison against the unchanged Tiny Aya Global base used 48 previously inspected synthetic cases with the same pinned inference-time revision and decoding settings. The adapter increased complete JSON validity from 14/48 to 48/48 and raw expected-source identifier presence from 31/39 to 39/39 answerable cases. However, strict supported-answer matches remained 0/39; correct unsupported-question abstentions fell from 4/9 to 2/9, with unsupported claims rising from 5/9 to 7/9. We therefore do not claim model safety or factual improvement. A separate deterministic inference-time guard improved formatting and abstention in a CPU replay, but cannot establish that cited passages entail the generated answer.

We provide public synthetic data and dataset collection, reproducible source code, research documentation, and provenance hashes. Original model outputs and the trained adapter remain private where rights and release conditions are unresolved. Independent multilingual semantic review, a relevant public model collection, and official Lexsi submission remain outstanding.
